# Behavior, caching, and privacy

## What Joydash shows

The selected Firefox bookmark folder controls both the shortcuts shelf and the history results. Joydash shows recent pages only from hosts bookmarked directly in that folder. The shelf contains direct website bookmarks as icon-only tiles in one horizontally scrollable row. Choose **Arrange** to drag shortcuts into a new order, hide shortcuts, or restore hidden shortcuts. This only changes the local shelf layout; it does not change which bookmarked sites are included in history. Add or remove bookmarks in Firefox, or choose another folder, to change the available shortcuts. If a site moves to a different domain, update its bookmark URL so Joydash can match visits on the new host.

The **Continue where you left off** section can be collapsed; its visibility preference is stored locally. Use **Arrange sections** to drag Recent history, Random previews, and Playlists into a new order; the Up/Down controls are also available. Reordering is a draft until **Save order** is selected; **Close** dismisses the arranger without applying the draft. The saved order is stored locally. On reload, the section stack remains hidden until the order and initial history data are ready, then sections appear one at a time from top to bottom in the saved order. Drag sorting animates rows only when their order changes. The right-side History list and bottom shortcuts shelf stay in place. The history grid and side History list share search, site, and date filters; date ranges include 3 hours, 8 hours, 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days. **Videos only** and **No preview** affect the grid only: video-only mode is on by default, and video pages without a usable poster are hidden unless **No preview** is enabled. The History list shows matching pages from bookmarked sites. Its info button explains which folder and filters it uses, and its heading shows the local date and time instead of a page count. **Clear** removes the text query; **Reset filters** restores the default filters. **View more** searches farther back in history, and each per-site **Load more** can fetch older history when the currently loaded entries run out. These controls keep paging through Firefox history until it is exhausted; they do not load the full history on startup. Trash buttons on Recent history, Random previews, and History rail hover previews immediately remove all visits to that exact URL from Firefox history; saved playlist entries are not removed. Newly loaded posters in the recent-video grid fade in smoothly as they appear.

Open **Dashboard settings** from the settings icon to turn the floating Home button on or off, configure discreet mode, arrange dashboard sections, or clear saved poster art. The floating Home button appears on websites only while Firefox is in F11 full-screen mode and **Allow all sites** is enabled. With **Cover favorite website pages too** enabled, discreet mode also covers HTTP(S) pages on hosts in the selected bookmark folder. **Apply discreet mode in** controls whether the cover and favorite-page controls work in F11 full-screen mode, windowed mode, or both. On favorite pages, a hide icon can cover the current page; revealing Joydash or a favorite page reveals all covers until Firefox closes. This option needs **Allow all sites** so the extension can add its cover to favorite pages. When discreet mode is enabled, Joydash opens covered the first time it loads each Firefox session. Reveal the dashboard to use it; it stays revealed until Firefox closes. Clearing poster art asks for confirmation and reports how many posters will be removed; if there is no saved art, nothing is changed. Bookmark-folder choices show each folder's name without its parent path. Opening a link from Joydash closes all Joydash dashboard tabs only while Firefox is in F11 full-screen mode, so returning Home does not leave duplicate dashboards.

**Random previews** selects from saved posters across the loaded history range, rather than limiting the slideshow to a day, and advances to new pairs every five seconds with a slow crossfade. Its **Next previews** arrow searches older history for more posters after the current pool has been shown. The top arrows can revisit up to three previous pairs; opening a preview removes any pair containing it from that short history. When a direct clip is available and reduced motion is off, it plays muted for that interval. Both cards pause when the section is off-screen, the document is hidden, or the dashboard is covered. Reduced-motion mode keeps the slow opacity fades but disables clips and scaling motion. Cards use the same hover and keyboard-focus highlight as other previews. If a clip cannot be played, Joydash shows that page's saved poster instead. Use **Save** on a verified video preview in Recent history or Random previews to add its page to a playlist. The History rail also offers this action from the hover preview when Joydash recognizes the page as a video.

The discreet cover replaces the dashboard with a neutral screen and pauses the random reel. It is activated automatically on the first dashboard opening of each Firefox session when enabled, and can also be activated manually with **Cover Joydash** while in the selected window mode. **Show Joydash** reveals it for the rest of the session; **Discreet settings** can disable the feature across browser restarts. Neither control requires additional permissions.

The side panel groups entries by site, newest first. It starts with five entries per site; **Load more** and **Show less** expand or collapse a site's list in groups of five. Hover or keyboard-focus an entry to see its saved poster or request a generic page image. Generic hover images are cached in memory only.

## Permissions and network requests

Joydash reads bookmarks and browsing history locally. Firefox 140 or later is required for its built-in data-consent prompt. The extension declares bookmark information, browsing activity, and website content because it reads this data locally and requests pages and preview images from approved origins.

Optional host permissions are requested as needed. An **Allow** button can open Firefox's permission prompt for a page or image host; permission is never requested automatically just because you hover. **Allow all sites** requests broad `<all_urls>` access. With that permission, Joydash adds a small home button to HTTP(S) pages while Firefox is in F11 full-screen mode; the button does not read page content and returns the current tab to Joydash. Even with that permission, Joydash limits preview requests to bookmarked sites in the selected history folder and their discovered image hosts, and requests favicons only from bookmarked origins. Third-party favicon services are not used. You can revoke permissions in Firefox's Add-ons Manager.

Preview page and image requests omit cookies and referrers, reject redirects, and are limited to 512 KB per page and 5 MB per image. For a known redirected favorite, Joydash can separately verify the redirect with a cookie-free, no-referrer ranged GET, then request the confirmed destination page without following redirects. **Load previews** can make a redirect check for direct bookmarks in the selected folder. **Check favorite redirects** also searches history using favorite-domain tokens, verifies up to 12 distinct unmatched origins against current favorites, and includes up to 500 supplemental pages total from known prior domains in Continue history. This allows older history beyond the normal recent-history window to remain visible. Redirect checks cancel the response body. Joydash updates a bookmark in place only after confirming a cross-host HTTP(S) redirect, with no HTTPS downgrade and with existing access to the destination. It retains up to ten prior bookmark origins locally so history from old domains remains associated with the same favorite. It does not update bookmarks for ordinary preview failures or request new host permissions automatically. Redirect checks may disclose bookmarked or unmatched history URLs and your IP address to the contacted sites. No data is sent to the developer. Joydash does not request tab or cookie access.

The reel recognizes direct MP4, WebM, and Ogg sources exposed by a page's `<video>` or `<source>` elements; embedded players, encrypted streams, and script-only media URLs are not supported. Source checks are deferred until the reel is visible, check at most eight saved-video pages at a time, and skip pages with a recent check. Playback is attempted only if the media host already has permission; Joydash never requests new host permissions for a clip. The video element is muted, uses anonymous CORS and a no-referrer policy, and is unloaded after each five-second interval. The browser may buffer more data than is displayed during that interval, and some hosts may block playback; in either case the poster remains the fallback.

## Local history and preview cache

Joydash stores a local snapshot of up to 500 recent pages from bookmarked sites in the selected history folder. Firefox history queries return up to 500 entries at a time; the app follows an older-time cursor as the user requests more, so 500 is a page size rather than a total history limit. On reload, the history snapshot normally checks Firefox for newer visits; it fully reconciles history at least daily and after folder/site changes or history removals. Older pages are not eagerly loaded or added to the persistent snapshot.

The selected playlist, video playlists, and discreet-mode opt-out are stored locally in Firefox; the active cover and preview navigation history are kept only for the current browser session. Playlist entries keep a page URL and title and are stored separately from poster art. Playlists show two rows initially and add two rows each time **View more** is selected. When the Playlists section becomes visible, Joydash automatically tries saved preview metadata and then the saved page for displayed items missing posters, using only site access already granted to Firefox. **Load missing previews** retries that process on demand. Up to 16 posters are retained locally; additional recovered playlist posters are kept only for the current dashboard session and may be fetched again later. **Clear saved art** removes cached and session-only posters but leaves playlists and preview metadata intact; the playlist section will not automatically restore posters after a clear. Joydash does not track time spent on pages or video playback.

Video classifications, preview-image URLs, and detected direct video URLs are cached for up to seven days, with up to 250 entries. Up to 16 resized posters are retained locally; posters for saved playlist entries are prioritized, then other available posters are sampled across the loaded history range and site origins where possible. **Clear saved art** removes poster images but keeps history and preview metadata. Older posters may refresh from approved sites while the saved copy remains visible.

Image candidates with larger declared dimensions are preferred. Images are resized to at most 960 × 600, and each saved poster is capped at 400 KB. A site may not provide a usable or high-resolution image, and a poster is not guaranteed to be an actual video frame.
